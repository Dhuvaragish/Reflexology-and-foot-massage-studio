Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Answers for Your First Visit

Everything you need to know about traditional reflexology and our studio policies.

While a foot massage focuses primarily on soothing muscular tightness and promoting relaxation in the feet and calves, foot reflexology is a structured modality based on zone therapy. Reflexologists stimulate specific nerve endings and meridian pathways that correspond to internal organs, glands, and the nervous system.
Reflexology should never cause sharp or agonizing pain. Certain reflex zones that correspond to congested organs may feel slightly tender or produce a dull "sweet ache" as tension is dispersed. Our therapists constantly communicate and adjust their pressure according to your preference: gentle, medium, or therapeutic firm.
We recommend wearing comfortable, loose-fitting trousers or joggers that can easily be rolled up past your knees so your practitioner can access your calves and lower leg meridians. We also provide clean, comfortable studio loungewear if you are arriving directly from work.
Hygiene is our highest priority. All foot basins are fitted with single-use eco-friendly liners or undergo hospital-grade medical autoclave sanitization between guests. All linens and towels are laundered at high thermal sanitization levels, and therapists thoroughly wash and sanitize hands before every treatment phase.
