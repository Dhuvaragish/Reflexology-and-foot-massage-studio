Our Origins

Rooted in Tradition, Refined for Today

Solace & Sole was born from a fundamental observation: in modern living, our connection to the ground beneath our feet has been severed by synthetic shoes, concrete pavements, and sedentary hours behind screens.

When our founders studied Traditional Chinese Medicine in Taiwan and Wat Pho bodywork in Thailand, they discovered that regular acupressure applied to the foot's 7,200 nerve endings is not simply a pleasant luxury—it is an indispensable preventive medicine that normalizes autonomic nerve function, improves digestion, and settles restless minds.

We built our studio as an organic acoustic sanctuary where every design choice—from natural cedar and slate to gentle bamboo lanterns and seasonal loose-leaf teas—works in quiet harmony to slow your breathing and return you to balance.

Clinical Cleanliness

The Five Pillars of Studio Hygiene

Your health and peace of mind are non-negotiable. We maintain rigorous medical-grade sanitation protocols across every treatment suite, basin, and tool.

1. Autoclave & Tool Sterilization

All wooden reflexology styluses, basalt stones, and acupoint tools are cleaned with medical-grade hospital disinfectants and UV sterilized between every single client.

2. Single-Use Eco Basin Liners

Each herbal foot soak tub is fitted with an individual, biodegradable protective liner that is disposed of after use, completely eliminating any risk of cross-contamination.

3. Thermal Laundry Sanitization

100% Egyptian cotton towels, fleece table blankets, and headrest linens undergo commercial high-temperature washing (75°C / 167°F) with hypoallergenic plant detergents.

4. Medical HEPA Air Purification

Every private suite is equipped with quiet, continuous true-HEPA 13 medical filtration with activated carbon to remove 99.97% of airborne allergens and dust.

5. Therapist Health Protocols

Practitioners undergo surgical scrubbing before and after each session. Therapists with any respiratory symptoms are relieved of duties with full paid sick leave.

Certified Clean Seal

Audited quarterly by the State Board of Massage Therapy & Holistic Practitioners with a perfect 100% compliance rating since our opening.

Ethical Craft

Cold-Pressed Oils & Organic Herbal Infusions

We never use mineral oils, artificial parabens, or synthetic perfumes on your skin.

Every drop of oil that touches your soles is cold-pressed, non-comedogenic, and derived from organic sweet almond, golden jojoba, and raw sesame seed. Our medicinal foot soaks utilize hand-harvested wild mugwort (Ai Ye), dried ginger root (Gan Jiang), and Epsom salt crystals enriched with trace minerals.

Following your session, you are welcomed to linger in our tea lounge to enjoy complimentary seasonal herbal infusions, formulated to support digestive and kidney function.
