Treatment Overview

Our signature Eastern Foot Reflexology begins with a warm herbal salt foot bath infused with lemongrass and ginger. Your certified reflexologist uses precise finger, thumb, and wooden stylus pressure techniques along your foot's 7,200 nerve endings. By stimulating specific reflex zones corresponding to vital organs, glands, and the musculoskeletal system, this treatment triggers endorphin release, stimulates lymph drainage, and fosters profound whole-body homeostasis.

Core Clinical & Wellness Benefits

Alleviates plantar fascia tightness and arch discomfort
Stimulates blood circulation and lymphatic drainage
Promotes deep delta-wave sleep and reduces nocturnal insomnia
Balances sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems

Step-by-Step Session Journey

Phase 1: Cleansing Soak

Floral foot wash & ginger-salts soak in traditional cedar basin (10m)

Phase 2: Meridian Analysis

Reflex zone tactile warm-up and palpation to identify energetic blockages

Phase 3: Targeted Acupressure

Meridian thumb-walking and wooden stylus pressure-point balancing across all reflex zones

Phase 4: Lower Leg & Towel Wrap

Calf & lower leg lymphatic drainage stroking followed by warm steamed herbal towel wrap

Preparation Advice

Please arrive 10 minutes prior to enjoy warm herbal tea. Wear loose trousers that can be rolled to the knee. Avoid heavy meals within 1 hour before treatment.

Aftercare Guidance

Drink 2 to 3 glasses of warm mineral water over the next few hours to flush metabolic byproducts. Rest your feet and avoid intense workouts today.

Health & Medical Contraindications

First trimester of pregnancy, acute deep vein thrombosis (DVT), severe foot fractures or open fungal infections.
